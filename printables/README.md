# Limn: printed parts

Every printed part of Limn, a pen plotter with a toolchanger, cut out of the Fusion export `Limn-Export-42 v88.step`
by `guide/export.py`. One STEP and one STL per part, in mm, placed as modelled: lay them flat in the slicer.

**92 parts, 115 prints.** Mirrored parts are files of their own.
Parts of several bodies (the diffusers, the icons, the inlays on the tool brackets) are multi-colour: keep
their bodies together and give each its filament.

## Hardware

From the project's [component list](https://hackaday.io/project/205431/components).

| Qty | Part | For |
|---:|---|---|
| 2 | NEMA 17 Stepper Motor | CoreXY A and B axes |
| 1 | 10mm Linear Micro Stepper Motor | K axis: the toolchanger key |
| 1 | NEMA 8 Stepper Motor | Z axis |
| 6 | M2x6 SHSS Machine Screw | Form the toolchanger docking surface |
| 3 | M2.5x5 Button Head Screw | The kinematic coupling triple |
| 6 | 4x2 mm round magnet | On the SHSS screws, to pull the coupling triple in |
| 12 | 10x5x1 mm rectangular magnet | 2 per tool, 2 per tool holder, several for the bed |
| 1 | KFC-W-13W Micro Switch | Z axis endstop (up) |
| 6 | Reset detection micro switch | AliExpress find: small switches with screw holes. Tool presence in the holders, and the key. |
| 1 | Klipper MCU (reclaimed) | A Melzi board for now, with 4 stepper drivers, from the same old printer as the frame |
| 1 | BLTouch Z probe |  |
| 1 | MCP23017 I2C breakout | Reads the tool holders' switches |
| 1 | PN532 RFID breakout | Reads the tools' tags |
| 1 | Transistor OR gate | DIY: merges two BLTouch-like probe signals |
| 1 | Resistive touch screen panel | Automatic offset calibration (soon optional) |
| 1 | FS-ARR-4X8-ROT | 4x8 force sensitive resistor, for precise automatic offset calibration |
| 1 | CoreXY frame (reclaimed) | Built from an old 3D printer + a 3D printed framework |
| 3 | Waveshare RP2040 | Thin enough to sit in the tool calibration bed |
| 1 | RP2040 MCU | Extra GPIO for the LEDs and the buzzer |
| 1 | Limn Z axis | Several designs printed, still evolving |
| 1 | Pogo pins, 5 mm | Data between the tool and the plotter |
| 1 | Raspberry Pi 4 | Runs Klipper |
| 1 | USB UVC cameras | Debugging and monitoring |
| 1 | USB-C 65 W power supply | Powers the Pi, and the plotter through a 12 V trigger cable |
| 1 | Power switch | For emergencies |
