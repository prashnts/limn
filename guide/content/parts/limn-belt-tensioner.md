- An earlier tensioner moved inline with the belt. Its geometry was off, and it wore the belt.
- In the backpack, one end of each belt is tensioned at the toolhead, and the other ends are held by the backpack ([log #15](/log/2026-09-05-loose-belts.html)).
- Tracks hold the belt clips in place, and their chamfers make it easier to put a clip in under tension.
- [i] It's small, so pre-tension the belt before fitting the clip.
- Print the [[limn-t41-tensioner-body]] at an angle: it makes the small screw holes stronger.
- [i] Loose belts made the docking miss, which looked like a dock calibration problem.
