## Hardware per pen, from the build notes

| Part | Qty | For |
|---|---|---|
| M2.5 button-head screws | 3 | the coupling, adjusted per tool |
| 5 × 10 × 1 mm magnets | 2 | the other way round from the dock's |
| 10 × 20 mm NFC tag | 1 | the pen's offsets and name |
| BT2.5 × 10 | 2 | the key hole |
| BT2.5 × 5, 8 or 10 | 2–4 | clamp the pen |

- [i] Sticking the tag on the outside got it rubbed off in the dock: about 20 tools were already printed by then. The dock holders now leave room for it. Putting the tag inside the tool would be better.
- Variants exist for the Stabilo ([[plot-tool-bracket-stabilo]]) and the Staedtler ([[plot-tool-bracket-staedtler]]) pens, and there's a [[plot-cutter-tool]].
