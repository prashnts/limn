## What's here

- **[The machine](/machine.html):** every design in the CAD as it's assembled, from the whole plotter down to a
  single chain link. Each one has a page with a render, its model to turn around and its STEP file.
- **[Parts](/parts/index.html):** the same designs as a gallery, by kind: printed, bought, cut from stock, reclaimed.
- **[Bill of materials](/bom.html):** what to buy and what to print, with counts from the CAD.
- **[The log](/log/index.html):** how it was built, as it happened. The Hackaday build logs are mirrored here, with the
  write-ups from the repository's notebooks.
- **[How it was made](/story.html)**, **[Electronics](/electronics.html)** (with the wiring drawings),
  and **[Software](/software.html)**. The story ends with [the CAD over time](/story.html#the-cad-over-time).

The pages are built from the Fusion 360 export (`guide/build.py` reads the STEP file), so they follow the design:
export again, build again.
