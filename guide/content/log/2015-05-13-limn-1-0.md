The first Limn, from a robotics lab at Delhi University, during Prashant's undergrad, where the work was building RAMPS and RepRap 3D printers. The name (*limn*: to draw, to depict) was chosen for a plotter there, and this calibration run is from 2015.

Eleven years later the plotter on this site carries the name forward as Limn 2.0, on the same DIY motto: [how it was made](/story.html).
